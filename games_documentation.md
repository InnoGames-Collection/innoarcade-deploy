## Ball Shooter (ቦል ሹተር)

**Description:** Ball Shooter is an engaging arcade and skill game. Shoot orbiting targets and clear waves. Dive in for an entertaining experience that challenges your skills and keeps you coming back for more.

**How to Play:**
1. Tap Play — your shooter sits at the bottom.
2. Tap or drag to aim at colored orbs on the track.

4. Survive waves and chain hits for a high score.

**Scoring Logic:**
This is a skill-based game where every action counts. You will need to reach a specific score milestone to secure a win and claim your rewards. ## 2048 (2048)

**Description:** 2048 is an engaging puzzle and casual game. Merge tiles to reach 2048 and beyond. Dive in for an entertaining experience that challenges your skills and keeps you coming back for more.

**How to Play:**
1. Swipe up, down, left, or right to slide all tiles.
